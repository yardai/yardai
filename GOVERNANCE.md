# Governance

YardAI is fully open source (Apache-2.0) and run transparently: decisions, roadmaps, experiments, training runs and contribution records are public.

## Roles

- **Contributors** — anyone who has had a contribution merged or accepted.
- **Maintainers** — contributors with merge rights in one or more areas. Nominated by existing maintainers after sustained, high-quality contributions.
- **Founder / lead maintainer** — Rahul Garg, who sets direction during the bootstrap phase and breaks ties.

## Decision making

- Day-to-day changes: one maintainer approval on a PR.
- Protocol, architecture, licensing or governance changes: an RFC in `docs/rfcs/`, open for comment for at least 7 days, then accepted or rejected by maintainers with written reasoning.
- All decisions and their reasoning live in the repository, never only in private chat.

## Founding-contributor ownership program

YardAI intends to share ownership of the company with the people who build it:

> **The top 5 contributors will each own 10% of the company.**

### How contributions will be measured

The goal is a measure that is public, hard to game, and rewards impact rather than volume. The working proposal:

- A public, append-only **contribution ledger** in the repository, updated as PRs merge.
- Contributions are weighted by impact (e.g. core protocol work, research that lands in training, reviews, docs), not by lines of code or commit count.
- Weights and the scoring method are themselves decided through the RFC process, so anyone can see and challenge them.

### Important: this is a statement of intent

This program is **not yet a legal agreement**, and nothing in this repository is an offer of securities or a binding promise of equity. Before anyone relies on it, the project will publish — and finalise with legal counsel — the details that make it real, including:

- the legal entity that holds the equity, and its jurisdiction;
- the measurement window (when the "top 5" is determined) and the scoring rules;
- vesting, dilution and what happens if a contributor leaves;
- eligibility (e.g. sanctions and local securities law), and tie-breaking.

Until then, contributing under Apache-2.0 with DCO sign-off is what governs your contribution. These open questions are tracked in [todo.md](todo.md).
